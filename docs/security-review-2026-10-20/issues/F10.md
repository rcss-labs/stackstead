## Problem and impact

Twenty operations per stack, 20,000 lines per operation and about 8 KiB per line permit theoretical payload retention around 156 MiB/operation and 3.05 GiB/stack before overhead. Completed histories expire only under same-stack replacement; removed stacks and unique-name churn lack a global lifetime bound. No OOM/heap measurement was performed. This merges security SEC-07 rather than creating a duplicate.

Priority: **P3**. Also tracks **SEC-07**.
