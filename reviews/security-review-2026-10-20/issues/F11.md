## Problem and impact

Fleet polls and hello/checker calls create transports and close idle connections repeatedly, losing connection reuse and repeating TCP/TLS setup. Forwarding already caches per-peer transports. Latency impact is not measured.

Priority: **P3**.

## Proposed work

Measure handshake/request overhead, then consider a bounded shared client cache with trust/address invalidation, removal cleanup, shutdown cleanup and request-specific deadlines.
