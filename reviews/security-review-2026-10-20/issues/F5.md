## Problem and impact

The shared dirty guard excludes browser Back/Forward, and new-stack creation lacks the unload handling used by some detail editors. Users can lose a dirty draft on Back or reload without confirmation.

Priority: **P2**.

Provide shared history-navigation blocking and dirty-state unload handling. Avoid adding another independent partial click guard.
