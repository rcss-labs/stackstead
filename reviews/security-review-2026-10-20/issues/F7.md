## Problem and impact

Open concatenates a raw path into a file: URI while OpenExisting escapes %, ? and #. Valid data-directory characters can be interpreted as URI syntax, causing failure or unexpected placement, while backup/admin commands look for the literal filename. Arbitrary remote database compromise is not established.

Priority: **P2**.
