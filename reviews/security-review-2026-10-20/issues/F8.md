## Problem and impact

db init can migrate without the app maintenance lock. A newer CLI can migrate an older running server's schema or race restore publication. Same-version ordinary initialization alone does not establish the hazardous outcome; SQLite transaction locks do not replace maintenance exclusion.

Priority: **P2**.
